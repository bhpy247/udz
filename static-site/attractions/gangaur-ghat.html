Gangaur Ghat

Vibrant historic stepped ghat on Pichola, central to Udaipur's religious festivities.

About this Landmark

The most iconic bathing and ceremonial ghat on the eastern shore of Lake Pichola, right behind Bagore Ki Haveli. Site of colourful festivities during the annual Gangaur and Mewar festivals, and a beloved backdrop for photographers throughout the year.
